# About

I help people find the real problem.

Sometimes it's a customer who sees an error. Sometimes it's a developer who knows something is wrong but can't figure out where to look. My job is to figure out what's actually happening — not what someone thinks is happening.

## How I work

The most important step in any investigation is making sure I understand the problem correctly. Get this wrong, and everything after it is wasted effort — at best you go in circles before finding the real issue, at worst you fix something that was never broken in the first place.

Once I'm confident about what we're chasing, I find the closest entry point to where the problem occurs. That almost always starts with logs — finding the timestamp, looking for an ID I can trace, something concrete to hold onto. From there I follow the trail. Sometimes it leads to the backend API. Sometimes to a mobile app. Sometimes to a third-party service. The approach is the same regardless of where the trail ends.

## What I bring

I unblock people. When a team is stuck on a production issue they can't reproduce or a bug that only happens in someone else's environment, I step in to isolate the root cause so the right people can fix it faster. A wrong diagnosis costs hours or days. Getting it right the first time saves everyone that wait.

I've spent the last 10+ years working across the stack — applications, APIs, databases, infrastructure, integrations — investigating whatever lands on my desk. I don't always own the code, but I own finding the answer.

## What I believe

Every investigation has a root cause. The hard part isn't finding it — it's not fooling yourself into thinking you found it before you actually did.
